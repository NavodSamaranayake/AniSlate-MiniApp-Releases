# AniSlate

**Your personal hub for ongoing anime — track what's airing, get notified the moment a new episode drops, and never lose your place in a season again.**

AniSlate is an Android companion for staying on top of seasonal and ongoing anime. Sign in securely with your Telegram account, and everything you follow syncs into one clean, fast app.

> 📦 **This repository hosts the official app downloads.** Grab the latest `.apk` from the **[Releases](../../releases)** page.

---

## What AniSlate does

### 🔔 Real-time episode alerts
Get a push notification the instant a new episode you care about is ready — even when the app is closed or in the background. No refreshing, no FOMO.

### 📅 Weekly airing schedule
See what's airing each day at a glance, with a clean schedule view so you always know when the next episode of your shows lands.

### 🔍 Instant search
Search across a huge catalog to find any show instantly, with posters, scores, and quick links to its latest available episode.

### 📣 Announcements
Catch important updates and announcements delivered straight to your device.

### 🎨 Beautiful, modern design
A polished Material You interface with full **light and dark theme** support, smooth animations, and a layout that just feels good to use.

### 🔐 Private by design
Sign in securely with Telegram — no passwords to create or remember. Your session stays on your device.

---

## Getting started

1. Open the **[Releases](../../releases)** page.
2. Download the latest `AniSlate-MiniApp-<version>-release.apk`.
3. Open the file and allow installation from unknown sources if prompted.
4. Launch AniSlate and sign in with Telegram.

> 💡 **Updating:** When a newer version is available, the app will let you know on launch. Just download and install the latest release — your data and settings are kept.
